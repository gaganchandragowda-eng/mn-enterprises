What we stand for

Three principles that have guided us since day one.

No fakes. Ever.

We buy directly from manufacturers. Every wire, pipe and pump on our shelf is genuine and ISI / ISO certified where applicable.

Fair price, not just cheap.

Our prices reflect bulk purchasing power — not compromised quality. We'd rather charge you the right price for a real product than the wrong price for a fake.

People behind the counter.

WhatsApp us a photo, call us, or walk in. There's always someone who knows the products and will give you a straight answer — not a sales pitch.

Open when you need us.

6:30 AM to 9:00 PM, 365 days. Pipe burst on a Sunday? We're open. Motor failed during harvest? We're open. Always.

Our journey

From a small stall to Bangarapet's most complete hardware shop.

2010
Shop opens on APMC Road
Started as a small electrical supplies stall, focusing on wires and switches for homes and small businesses in Bangarapet.
2013
Authorised KEI stockist
Became an authorised dealer for KEI Cables, bringing ISI-grade wiring directly to Kolar district at fair prices.
2016
Expanded to plumbing
Added PVC/CPVC pipes, Flowken storage tanks and full range of fittings — the first single-stop shop for both electrical and plumbing in the area.
2019
Sharp Pumps partnership
Authorised dealer for Sharp water pumps. Added submersible and self-priming pumps for agricultural and household use.
2021
CCTV & Networking added
Responded to growing demand for home security and WiFi — now stocking full CCTV kits, NVR systems, routers and networking cables.
2025
Online catalogue launched
This website — making it easier for customers across Kolar to browse stock, order on WhatsApp, and pick up the same day.
